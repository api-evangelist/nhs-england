# NHS England

> NHS England (which absorbed NHS Digital in 2023) runs England's national public health and social care API platform at digital.nhs.uk (developer portal) and api.service.nhs.uk (runtime). It publishes a catalogue of internet-facing APIs — most HL7 FHIR R4 built to the FHIR UK Core profile — spanning the national Spine services. Access is centrally governed: applications onboard through the NHS API platform and authenticate with OAuth 2.0 bearer tokens (application-restricted signed-JWT client credentials, or user-restricted via NHS login / NHS CIS2), with production gated behind registration, assurance and connection agreements.

This llms.txt was GENERATED by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the repo's apis.yml and harvested OpenAPI specs (the digital.nhs.uk docs host returns 403 to automated clients, so no upstream /llms.txt could be fetched).

## APIs
- [Personal Demographics Service (PDS) - FHIR](https://digital.nhs.uk/developer/api-catalogue/personal-demographics-service-fhir): National patient demographics (name, address, DOB, GP, nominated pharmacy, NHS number) as FHIR R4. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/personal-demographics/FHIR/R4
- [GP Connect Access Record - Structured - FHIR](https://digital.nhs.uk/developer/api-catalogue/gp-connect-access-record-structured-fhir): Retrieve a patient's GP record as structured FHIR from EMIS and TPP SystmOne.
- [Electronic Prescription Service (EPS) - FHIR](https://digital.nhs.uk/developer/api-catalogue/electronic-prescription-service-fhir): Send electronic prescriptions prescriber-to-dispenser as FHIR R4. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/electronic-prescriptions/FHIR/R4
- [e-Referral Service (e-RS) - FHIR](https://digital.nhs.uk/developer/api-catalogue/e-referral-service-fhir): Paperless referrals primary-to-secondary care, CRI and attachments. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/referrals/FHIR
- [Booking and Referral Standard (BaRS) - FHIR](https://github.com/NHSDigital/booking-and-referral-fhir-api): Booking and referral messages between NHS providers as FHIR R4. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/booking-and-referral/FHIR/R4
- [Immunisation History - FHIR](https://digital.nhs.uk/developer/api-catalogue/immunisation-fhir-api): Patient immunisation record (COVID-19, influenza, HPV) as FHIR R4. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/immunisation-history/FHIR/R4
- [Organisation Data Service (ODS) - FHIR](https://github.com/NHSDigital/organisation-data-service-fhir-api): Reference data on NHS organisations, roles and terminology as FHIR R4. Base: https://beta.ods.dc4h.link/fhir/
- [NHS App API](https://github.com/NHSDigital/nhs-app-api): Deliver messages and content to citizens and link into NHS App journeys. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/nhs-app
- [NHS Notify (Communications Manager)](https://github.com/NHSDigital/communications-manager-api): Send messages to people about their health and care (NHS App, SMS, email, letter). Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/comms
- [Service Search (Directory of Healthcare Services)](https://github.com/NHSDigital/service-search-api): Search the national Directory of Healthcare Services. Base: https://api.service.nhs.uk/service-search-api
